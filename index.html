Laundry is the process of washing, drying, and caring for clothes, linens, and other fabrics to maintain cleanliness and hygiene. It is an essential household chore practiced worldwide and plays a vital role in personal grooming and public health. Traditionally done by hand, laundry has evolved with the invention of washing machines, dryers, and various cleaning agents that make the process more efficient and effective. Proper laundry techniques help preserve the quality, color, and texture of fabrics, ensuring that garments last longer and remain comfortable to wear. Understanding the basics of sorting, washing, drying, and ironing is key to successful laundry care.
